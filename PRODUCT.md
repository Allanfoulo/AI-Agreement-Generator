# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners, administrators, freelancers, agencies, consultants, service companies, small teams, and nonprofit operators who create repeatable business documents. They work in a focused desktop workspace and need to verify facts before anything becomes authoritative.

## Product Purpose

Brief Doc X turns company, client, employee, and service records into professional quotes, invoices, employee letters, and service-level agreements. The product should feel like a calm document studio, not an accounting system or an opaque AI chatbot.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, warm.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic indigo SaaS dashboards, dense accounting software, chatbot-first interfaces, decorative glass panels, and AI-generated document markup. The reference is inspiration for warmth, framing, and structure, not a visual clone.

## Design Principles

- Structured facts stay visible and authoritative.
- AI assistance should feel like a capable collaborator, never an unreviewable oracle.
- Every screen should make the next document action obvious.
- Warmth comes from color, rhythm, and copy, not visual clutter.
- Familiar controls earn trust; personality belongs in the framing and moments of feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic headings, descriptive labels, and status announcements for asynchronous saves and PDF jobs.
